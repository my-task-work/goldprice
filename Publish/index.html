How Does Emirates NBA Cup 2026 Group Play Work?

A clear explanation of the first stage of the NBA’s in-season tournament

Group Play is the foundation of the Emirates NBA Cup. It is the opening stage that determines which teams advance to the high-stakes Knockout Rounds. Understanding how Group Play works helps fans follow their favorite teams, track standings, and know exactly what is at stake during the early weeks of the regular season.

What Is Group Play?

Group Play is the first stage of the Emirates NBA Cup. All 30 NBA teams are divided into groups and compete against one another in a limited number of games. The results of these contests decide which teams move forward into the single-elimination Knockout Rounds and which teams are eliminated from Cup contention.

Purpose of Group Play

The main purpose of Group Play is to create a fair and competitive path for teams to qualify for the later stages of the tournament. It gives every franchise a chance to compete for advancement while still counting the games toward the regular-season standings. By concentrating many of these matchups on designated “Cup Nights,” the league also creates a distinct mid-season event atmosphere.

How the first stage works

Teams are placed into balanced groups using a pot system based on the previous season’s records. Each team then plays a fixed number of games against the other members of its group. At the end of Group Play, the top teams from each group, plus two wild cards, advance. The remaining teams are eliminated from the Emirates NBA Cup but continue their normal regular-season schedules.

How Many Teams Are in Each Group?

  • Six groups in total
  • Five teams in each group
  • Three groups in the Eastern Conference
  • Three groups in the Western Conference

This structure keeps the groups conference-based while ensuring a manageable number of games. The league uses a pot system so that each group contains one team from every performance tier of the prior season, preventing any group from becoming too strong or too weak.

How Many Games Does Each Team Play?

Every team plays exactly four Group Play games.

  • One game against each of the other four teams in its group
  • Two home games
  • Two away games

This balanced home-and-road schedule ensures that no team gains a significant venue advantage within its group. All four games count fully toward the team’s regular-season win-loss record.

When Are Group Play Games Played?

Cup Nights

Most Group Play contests are scheduled on designated “Cup Nights.” These special dates help create a focused tournament feel and make it easier for fans to follow the action. In 2026, the main Cup Nights fall primarily on Fridays, with two additional midweek dates in late November.

Group Play schedule

The 2026 Group Play schedule includes games on:

  • Friday, October 30
  • Friday, November 6
  • Friday, November 13
  • Friday, November 20
  • Tuesday, November 24
  • Wednesday, November 25
  • Friday, November 27

Number of Group Play games

Across the entire Group Play stage, each of the 30 teams plays four games, resulting in a total of 60 Group Play contests (30 teams × 4 games ÷ 2).

How Are Group Standings Determined?

Standings within each group are ranked primarily by wins and losses. The team with the best record in its group finishes first. When teams finish with identical records, point differential (points scored minus points allowed) and other tiebreakers are applied as needed to determine final positions.

Position within the group is critical: the first-place team automatically advances, while strong second-place finishes can earn a wild-card berth.

How Does a Team Advance?

Advancement from Group Play follows a clear formula.

Group winner

The team that finishes with the best record in each of the six groups automatically qualifies for the Knockout Rounds.

Conference wild card

One additional team from each conference also advances. This wild-card spot goes to the second-place team with the best Group Play record in that conference.

Eight total teams advancing

Six group winners + one Eastern Conference wild card + one Western Conference wild card = eight teams that move on to the Knockout Rounds.

This system keeps more teams competitive until the final Group Play games, because a strong second-place finish can still lead to qualification.

What Happens After Group Play?

After Group Play is completed, the eight qualifying teams move on to the Knockout Rounds, where every game is single elimination. The remaining teams are eliminated from the NBA Cup competition, although their games continue to count toward the regular season.

Quarterfinals

The eight advancing teams enter a single-elimination bracket. Quarterfinal games are scheduled for December 4–5.

Knockout format

From the quarterfinals onward, every game is win-or-go-home. There are no best-of series. The winners of the quarterfinals move to the semifinals, and the two semifinal winners meet in the championship.

Elimination from the tournament

Any team that loses in the Knockout Rounds is eliminated from the Emirates NBA Cup. Teams that fail to qualify from Group Play are also eliminated from Cup contention at that point, although they continue playing their remaining regular-season games.

Do Group Play Games Count as Regular-Season Games?

Yes. Every Group Play game counts fully as a regular-season contest. Wins and losses from these games appear in a team’s official 82-game record and affect standings, playoff seeding, and individual player statistics.

How NBA Cup games affect regular-season standings

Because Group Play, quarterfinal, and semifinal games all count, success (or struggles) in the Emirates NBA Cup can influence a team’s position in the conference standings for the rest of the season. Teams that advance deeper into the tournament effectively play additional meaningful games that count toward their regular-season totals.

Championship exception

The only game in the entire Emirates NBA Cup that does not count in the regular-season standings is the championship final itself. All other Cup contests — Group Play through the semifinals — are official regular-season games.

Group Play sets the stage for everything that follows in the Emirates NBA Cup. By understanding the group structure, the four-game schedule, the ranking system, and the advancement rules, fans can fully appreciate the early drama of the tournament and track which teams are best positioned to make a deep Cup run.
